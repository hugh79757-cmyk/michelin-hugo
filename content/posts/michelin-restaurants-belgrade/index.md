With a total of 24 Michelin-rated establishments, the city offers a mix of fine dining and casual eateries, each with its unique charm and flavors. The Michelin recognition here includes one prestigious star, three Bib Gourmand awards, and a selection of 20 restaurants that have been acknowledged for their quality without receiving a star. This guide will delve into the highlights of Belgrade's dining scene, exploring the standout restaurants and the various cuisines available.

At the pinnacle of Belgrade's Michelin offerings is **Langouste**, which has earned a coveted star for its modern and contemporary cuisine. This restaurant is ideally situated on the edge of the historic center, offering diners expansive picture windows that enhance the dining experience. The ambiance is refined, and the menu reflects a commitment to creativity and quality, with prices ranging from €70 to €150. Dining at Langouste is an experience characterized by meticulous attention to detail and a passion for innovative flavors.

## Bib Gourmand: Best Value Fine Dining

For those seeking exceptional value without compromising on quality, Belgrade's Bib Gourmand selections are noteworthy. **Istok**, a Vietnamese and Asian restaurant, is a budget-friendly option located conveniently close to the town center. With a focus on elegant presentation and authentic flavors, it offers a delightful dining experience for under €30. 

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-belgrade/body_3.jpg)
*Photo by [K](https://www.pexels.com/@kelly) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


Another Bib Gourmand recipient is **Iva New Balkan Cuisine**, a welcoming bistro known for its colorful, contemporary decor. This establishment also keeps its prices under €30, making it a popular choice for both locals and visitors looking to enjoy modern Balkan dishes in a relaxed setting.

**Bela Reka**, situated on the outskirts of the city center, presents traditional cuisine that emphasizes the quality of its ingredients. With a moderate price range of €30 to €70, this restaurant is ideal for those who appreciate hearty, authentic Serbian dishes.

## Cuisine Styles You Will Find in Belgrade

Belgrade's culinary landscape is rich and varied, featuring a range of cuisines that reflect both local traditions and international influences. Modern cuisine is well represented, with five restaurants focusing on innovative dishes that highlight seasonal ingredients. Notable among these is **Homa**, which offers a creative dining experience in the historic Dorćol quarter, although it is positioned at the higher end of the price spectrum, exceeding €150.

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-belgrade/body_4.jpg)
*Photo by [Boris Hamer](https://www.pexels.com/@borishamer) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


For those interested in traditional flavors, several establishments excel in this area. **Bela Reka** and **Klub Književnika by Branko Kisic** serve up traditional Balkan dishes, while **Suvenir** combines classic cuisine with a unique riverside setting.

Italian and Mediterranean options are also available, with **S5 by Angie** and **Comunale Caffè e Cucina** providing delicious pasta and seafood dishes that cater to a variety of tastes. The presence of Asian influences is evident in **Ebisu**, which specializes in Japanese cuisine, and **Istok**, offering Vietnamese flavors.

## Price Ranges and What to Expect

Dining in Belgrade can cater to various budgets, with establishments falling into three main price categories. At the higher end, **Langouste** and **Homa** represent the expensive tier, where guests can expect a sophisticated dining experience characterized by exquisite presentation and innovative dishes. 

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-belgrade/body_5.jpg)
*Photo by [Boris Hamer](https://www.pexels.com/@borishamer) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


The moderate price range, encompassing €30 to €70, includes a majority of the Michelin-selected restaurants. Places like **The Square**, **Gušti mora**, and **Legat 1903** offer a balance of quality and affordability, making them accessible for a wider audience. 

For budget-conscious diners, options such as **Iva New Balkan Cuisine** and **Mezestoran Dvorište** provide an opportunity to enjoy delicious meals without exceeding €30. These restaurants focus on delivering quality food in a casual and inviting atmosphere.

## How to Book and Tips for Dining

When planning a visit to one of Belgrade’s Michelin-rated establishments, it is advisable to make reservations in advance, especially for the more popular spots like **Langouste** and **Homa**. Many restaurants offer online booking options, which can simplify the process. 

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-belgrade/body_6.jpg)
*Photo by [Boris Hamer](https://www.pexels.com/@borishamer) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


When dining out, consider exploring the local specialties and seasonal offerings that many restaurants highlight on their menus. This not only enhances the culinary experience but also supports local producers and reflects the region's agricultural bounty. 

In summary, Belgrade’s Michelin dining scene presents a diverse range of options that cater to various tastes and budgets. Whether you are seeking a luxurious dining experience or a casual meal with authentic flavors, the city’s restaurants are ready to welcome you with open arms and exceptional dishes.
