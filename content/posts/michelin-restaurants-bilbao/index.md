The diversity of dining options reflects the city's commitment to both traditional and innovative cuisine, making it a noteworthy destination for food enthusiasts.

Among the standout dining experiences in Bilbao, the one-star restaurants offer a unique blend of creativity and tradition. **Ola Martín Berasategui** stands out with its modern and traditional cuisine, located alongside the river within the Tayko hotel. The setting is as impressive as the menu, which is priced at over €150, making it a special occasion venue.

**Nerua Guggenheim Bilbao** provides a distinctive experience by allowing diners to enjoy creative dishes within the iconic Guggenheim Museum. This restaurant, priced between €70 and €150, combines art and food in a way that enhances both.

For seafood lovers, **Zarate** is a worth trying, offering traditional dishes inspired by the sea, also within the €70 to €150 range. The passion for fishing is real in every dish, making it a solid representation of the region's culinary roots.

**Mina**, another one-star establishment, presents a creative and seasonal menu in a setting that reflects its commitment to continuous growth and innovation. The price point is also over €150, indicating a refined dining experience.

Lastly, **Islares** offers a minimalist approach to creative cuisine, located opposite the Guggenheim. With a focus on natural decor and an equally impressive menu, this restaurant also falls into the very expensive category.

## Bib Gourmand: Best Value Fine Dining

Bilbao's Bib Gourmand selections provide excellent value without compromising on quality. **Kimtxu** is a fusion restaurant that surprises with its Asian influences, offering a relaxed atmosphere and a price range of €30 to €70. This informal setting makes it a great spot for those looking to enjoy a casual yet flavorful meal.

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-bilbao/body_3.jpg)
*Photo by [Reinhard Bruckner](https://www.pexels.com/@bruggi) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


Another notable mention is **Los Fueros**, a Basque restaurant that embodies the classic features of Bilbao's dining scene. Priced moderately, it offers a taste of local tradition in a welcoming environment.

**Al Margen** champions simplicity in its traditional cuisine, making it a relaxed choice for diners seeking authentic flavors without the fuss. This restaurant also falls within the moderate price range.

## Cuisine Styles You Will Find in Bilbao

Bilbao's culinary landscape is rich with diverse styles that reflect its cultural heritage. Traditional cuisine dominates the scene, with eight restaurants dedicated to showcasing local flavors and ingredients. Basque cuisine is particularly prominent, with three establishments focusing on its unique offerings.

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-bilbao/body_4.jpg)
*Photo by [Michel Meuleman](https://www.pexels.com/@mime) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


Creative and modern cuisine also finds a place in Bilbao, with three restaurants exploring innovative approaches to cooking. Fusion cuisine, blending international influences with local ingredients, is represented by two restaurants, adding an exciting dimension to the dining options.

Seafood lovers will appreciate the dedicated establishments that highlight the region's maritime bounty, while other unique styles, such as Peruvian and pork-focused dishes, further enrich the culinary diversity.

## Price Ranges and What to Expect

In terms of pricing, Bilbao offers a range of options to suit various budgets. The very expensive category includes restaurants like **Ola Martín Berasategui**, **Mina**, and **Islares**, where guests can expect a premium dining experience with exceptional service and carefully crafted dishes.

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-bilbao/body_5.jpg)
*Photo by [Johan Van Geijl](https://www.pexels.com/@johan-van-geijl-408240449) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


The expensive range, represented by restaurants such as **Nerua Guggenheim Bilbao**, **Zarate**, and **Aitor Rauleaga**, provides a balance of quality and value, making it accessible for those looking to indulge without breaking the bank.

Moderately priced establishments, including **Kimtxu**, **Los Fueros**, and **Al Margen**, cater to diners who appreciate quality food in a more casual setting. This range ensures that everyone can find something to enjoy in Bilbao's lively dining scene.

## How to Book and Tips for Dining

When planning a dining experience in Bilbao, it's advisable to make reservations, especially for the one-star and Bib Gourmand restaurants, as they can fill up quickly. Many of these establishments offer online booking options, which can simplify the process.

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-bilbao/body_6.jpg)
*Photo by [Osviel Rodriguez Valdés](https://www.pexels.com/@osviel91) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


For those looking to explore the culinary scene further, consider visiting during off-peak hours to enjoy a more relaxed atmosphere. Engaging with the staff can also enhance the experience, as they often have valuable insights into the menu and can recommend dishes based on personal preferences.

 Bilbao's Michelin dining scene offers a rich mix of flavors and experiences. Whether you seek the elegance of a one-star restaurant or the comfort of a Bib Gourmand establishment, the city's culinary offerings are sure to satisfy any palate.
