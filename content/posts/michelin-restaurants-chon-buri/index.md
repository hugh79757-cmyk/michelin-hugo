With a total of 20 Michelin-rated establishments, including five Bib Gourmand selections and 15 selected restaurants, the city showcases a diverse array of culinary offerings. This guide delves into the highlights of Chon Buri's Michelin dining landscape, providing insights into the exceptional flavors and dining experiences that await.

The Bib Gourmand category celebrates restaurants that offer exceptional food at reasonable prices. Chon Buri boasts five such establishments, each presenting unique culinary experiences that reflect the local culture and flavors.

La Voi is a standout in the small eats category, where the owner meticulously prepares broth and toppings using premium beef. The clear and amber soup serves as a delightful base for a variety of accompaniments, making it a favorite for those seeking quality in a casual setting.

Lung Shall Kitchen offers a simple yet refined Thai dining experience. The intimate atmosphere enhances the enjoyment of their carefully crafted dishes, which highlight the freshness and quality of the ingredients used.

Pladids is known for its Thai samrap, or set menu, which includes jasmine rice cooked with Thai wild almond. This thoughtful combination showcases the restaurant's dedication to traditional flavors while providing a satisfying meal.

Krua Laew Tae R-Rom focuses on seafood and home-made curries, featuring a limited menu that emphasizes bold and rich flavors. The restaurant's commitment to quality ingredients ensures a memorable dining experience.

Lastly, Khao Lam Mae Khai Toon Klao has been a beloved dessert stall for over 60 years. Here, patrons can savor classic sweetened sticky rice, prepared in the traditional manner that has stood the test of time.

## Cuisine Styles You Will Find in Chon Buri

Chon Buri's culinary scene is primarily dominated by Thai cuisine, which is represented by eight Michelin-rated establishments. The city's offerings extend beyond traditional Thai food, encompassing a variety of styles that cater to diverse palates. 

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-chon-buri/body_3.jpg)
*Photo by [ivy](https://www.pexels.com/@ivy-2164651) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


Isan cuisine is featured prominently, with restaurants like Klang Na Pla Khao and Somtum Je Phai serving up authentic dishes that reflect the northeastern region's flavors. Seafood lovers can delight in the offerings from Khrua Ban Po Ta and Fish Club, where fresh catches are prepared with skill and creativity.

For those in search of something different, Indian by Nature offers a fine dining experience that highlights authentic Indian flavors, while La Cucina presents Italian cuisine in a serene tropical garden setting. Additionally, noodle enthusiasts can indulge in dishes from Noodles Soi 12 (Ban Suan) and Neon Boat Noodles, both of which serve traditional preparations with various toppings.

The presence of small eats and street food options, such as Khao Tom Ped Jek Tong and Jay Jew Talew Bin, further enriches the dining landscape, providing casual yet flavorful experiences that are integral to the local food culture.

## How to Book and Tips for Dining

Reservations are recommended for Michelin-rated establishments, particularly during peak dining times or weekends. While some restaurants may accept walk-ins, securing a table in advance ensures a smoother dining experience.

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-chon-buri/body_5.jpg)
*Photo by [Jonny Belvedere](https://www.pexels.com/@jonny-belvedere-1161164) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


When dining in Chon Buri, consider exploring the local specialties and seasonal offerings. Engaging with the staff can enhance your experience, as they often have insights into the best dishes to order. Additionally, be open to trying different styles of cuisine, as the city's diverse culinary scene is sure to provide something that appeals to every palate.

 Chon Buri's Michelin dining scene presents an exciting opportunity to explore the rich flavors and culinary traditions of Thailand. With a variety of restaurants to choose from, each offering their unique take on local and international cuisines, there is much to discover in this coastal city.
