With a total of 24 Michelin-rated restaurants, the city showcases a diverse range of culinary experiences that reflect both local traditions and innovative approaches. The Michelin Guide highlights six one-star restaurants, each offering a unique perspective on the flavors of Mendoza.

Among the standout establishments in Mendoza, the one-star restaurants offer a glimpse into the region's culinary artistry. 

Brindillas, awarded a star for its modern cuisine, is located in Vistalba. While the area may not be particularly attractive, the restaurant's offerings are anything but ordinary. Guests can expect to indulge in dishes that showcase contemporary techniques and local ingredients, with prices ranging from $70 to $150.

Zonda Cocina de Paisaje brings a creative twist to traditional cuisine, also earning a star for its thoughtful approach to sourcing ingredients. The restaurant emphasizes the journey from field to plate, ensuring that every dish resonates with the essence of Mendoza's agricultural bounty. This experience is similarly priced between $70 and $150.

Riccitelli Bistró, another one-star establishment, focuses on seasonal and innovative cuisine. The restaurant combines fine dining with an appreciation for local produce, inviting diners to enjoy a menu that changes with the seasons, all within the same price range of $70 to $150.

Angélica Cocina Maestra stands out for its creative offerings and is recognized as a very expensive dining option, with prices exceeding $150. Named in honor of Nicolás Catena Zapata's mother, this restaurant presents a sophisticated take on local ingredients and culinary traditions.

Casa Vigil, also priced above $150, is the creation of renowned wine expert Alejandro Vigil, often referred to as the 'Messi of Wine.' This establishment offers a contemporary and regional cuisine experience that highlights the synergy between food and wine.

Lastly, Azafrán, another one-star restaurant, invites guests to explore the true essence of Mendoza’s cuisine. This very expensive option, with prices exceeding $150, is known for its commitment to quality and regional flavors.

## Cuisine Styles You Will Find in Mendoza

The culinary landscape in Mendoza is marked by a variety of styles that reflect both traditional and contemporary influences. The top cuisines represented in the Michelin Guide include modern cuisine, traditional cuisine, and creative approaches to food. 

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-mendoza/body_3.jpg)
*Photo by [Lilian Sandoval](https://www.pexels.com/@lilian-sandoval-310270209) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


Modern cuisine is prominently featured, with six restaurants dedicated to this style, including Brindillas and Azafrán. These establishments often incorporate innovative techniques and presentations, pushing the boundaries of traditional Argentine fare.

Traditional cuisine is also well-represented, showcasing the rich heritage of Argentine cooking. Zonda Cocina de Paisaje and 1884 Francis Mallmann exemplify this style, focusing on the use of local ingredients and time-honored cooking methods.

Creative cuisine, as seen in restaurants like Angélica Cocina Maestra and Riccitelli Bistró, emphasizes artistic interpretation and unique flavor combinations. This approach allows chefs to express their individuality while honoring local traditions.

Additionally, there are options for those who appreciate meats and grills, with establishments like Abrasado and Fogón Cocina de Viñedo offering expertly prepared dishes that highlight Argentina's renowned beef.

## Price Ranges and What to Expect

Dining in Mendoza can vary significantly in terms of price, with options to suit different budgets. The Michelin-rated restaurants in the city are categorized into three main price ranges: moderate, expensive, and very expensive.

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-mendoza/body_4.jpg)
*Photo by [Martin.que](https://www.pexels.com/@martin-que-243128669) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


Moderate options, such as Anna Bistró and Los Bocheros, offer a more accessible dining experience with prices ranging from $30 to $70. These restaurants provide a welcoming atmosphere and traditional dishes that reflect the local cuisine.

Expensive establishments, including Brindillas and Renacer, fall within the $70 to $150 range. These restaurants typically offer a more refined dining experience, with a focus on quality ingredients and innovative presentations.

For those seeking a premium experience, very expensive options like Angélica Cocina Maestra and Casa Vigil exceed $150. These restaurants often provide an elevated atmosphere, exceptional service, and menus that celebrate the best of Mendoza’s culinary landscape.

## How to Book and Tips for Dining

When planning a visit to Mendoza’s Michelin-rated restaurants, it’s advisable to make reservations in advance, especially for the one-star establishments and very expensive options. Booking ahead ensures that you secure a table at your desired restaurant, particularly during peak tourist seasons.

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-mendoza/body_5.jpg)
*Photo by [Valentin Angel Fernandez](https://www.pexels.com/@vafphotos) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


Dress codes can vary, but many of the higher-end restaurants encourage smart casual attire. It’s always a good idea to check the specific restaurant’s guidelines when making a reservation.

Additionally, considering the pairing of local wines with your meal can enhance the overall dining experience. Mendoza is famous for its Malbec, and many restaurants offer curated wine lists that complement their menus.

 Mendoza’s Michelin-rated dining scene presents an impressive array of culinary experiences, from traditional Argentine flavors to modern interpretations. Whether you are indulging in a one-star gem or exploring the diverse cuisine styles, the region promises a rich culinary journey that reflects its unique heritage and lively agricultural landscape.
