With a total of 21 establishments recognized by the Michelin Guide, including two with a coveted star, the city offers a unique blend of traditional and contemporary Mexican cuisine. From casual eateries to upscale dining experiences, Oaxaca presents a culinary landscape that reflects its lively culture and history.

Among the standout restaurants in Oaxaca, two establishments have earned the prestigious Michelin star. **Los Danzantes Oaxaca** is a beloved spot that has been serving traditional Mexican cuisine for 25 years. With an extensive menu that showcases the flavors of the region, this restaurant is a testament to the enduring appeal of Oaxacan cooking. The atmosphere is both elegant and inviting, making it a perfect choice for a special occasion or a memorable meal.

Another noteworthy one-star restaurant is **Levadura de Olla Restaurante**, where the focus is on traditional Mexican dishes crafted with fresh, local ingredients. The restaurant’s breezy courtyard provides a charming setting for guests to enjoy their meals, whether for a leisurely lunch or an intimate dinner. The talent of the young chef shines through in each dish, creating a delightful dining experience that celebrates the essence of Oaxacan cuisine.

## Bib Gourmand: Best Value Fine Dining

For those seeking exceptional dining experiences without breaking the bank, the Bib Gourmand category features six restaurants that offer remarkable value. **La Olla** is a long-standing favorite that may appear unassuming from the outside, yet it delivers a menu filled with authentic Mexican flavors. Diners can expect a warm atmosphere and thoughtful dishes that highlight the best of local ingredients.

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-oaxaca/body_3.jpg)
*Photo by [Santiago López](https://www.pexels.com/@santiago-lopez-231601510) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


**Labo Fermento** stands out for its unique approach to Asian cuisine in Oaxaca. The chef-owner, Joseph Gilbert, brings a creative twist to the dining scene, making this establishment a refreshing addition to the local culinary landscape. Guests can enjoy innovative dishes that showcase the chef's expertise in fermentation and flavor.

**Xaok** offers a stylish dining environment and a menu that emphasizes contemporary Mexican cuisine. Located off a busy street, the restaurant's interior is both chic and comfortable, providing an ideal backdrop for enjoying a meal with friends or family. 

**Alfonsina** presents a culinary journey that takes diners through the heart of Oaxacan flavors. While the journey to find this restaurant may feel uncertain at times, the reward is a menu that showcases the best of traditional cooking, crafted with care and creativity.

At **Las Quince Letras**, the focus is on mole, a signature dish of the region. Here, Chef Celia Florián has created a menu that celebrates the complexity and richness of this beloved sauce, ensuring that every bite is a testament to Oaxacan culinary traditions.

**Tierra del Sol** is another Bib Gourmand restaurant that reflects the talent of Chef Olga Cabrera. Located near the historic center, this restaurant offers a menu that highlights traditional Oaxacan dishes, prepared with a modern touch. 

## Cuisine Styles You Will Find in Oaxaca

The culinary scene in Oaxaca is diverse, with a strong emphasis on traditional Mexican cooking. Among the 21 Michelin-rated restaurants, the top cuisines represented include Mexican, Traditional Cuisine, and Mexican, Contemporary, providing a wide range of options for diners. 

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-oaxaca/body_4.jpg)
*Photo by [Mikhail Nilov](https://www.pexels.com/@mikhail-nilov) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


For those interested in regional specialties, **Las Quince Letras** and **Tierra del Sol** focus on traditional Oaxacan dishes, while **Labo Fermento** introduces Asian influences, showcasing the city's openness to diverse culinary styles. 

The presence of contemporary and fusion cuisine is also notable, with restaurants like **Nois** and **Crudo** pushing the boundaries of traditional flavors. This blend of styles reflects the dynamic nature of Oaxaca's food culture, making it a fascinating destination for food enthusiasts.

## Price Ranges and What to Expect

When dining in Oaxaca, guests can expect a range of prices that cater to various budgets. The Michelin-rated restaurants span from budget-friendly options to more expensive dining experiences. For those looking for affordable yet quality meals, establishments like **Barbacoa Obispo Cocina Rural** and **Itanoní** offer delicious options for under $30. 

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-oaxaca/body_5.jpg)
*Photo by [Miguel González](https://www.pexels.com/@mikegles) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


Mid-range diners will find plenty of Bib Gourmand restaurants, where meals typically range from $30 to $70. Here, diners can enjoy thoughtfully prepared dishes that highlight local ingredients and traditional cooking techniques.

For a more upscale experience, one-star restaurants like **Los Danzantes Oaxaca** and **Levadura de Olla Restaurante** offer meals in the $30 to $150 range, while selected restaurants such as **Criollo** and **Crudo** can exceed $150 for a more lavish dining experience. 

## How to Book and Tips for Dining

Reservations are recommended for most Michelin-rated restaurants, especially during peak dining hours or busy seasons. Guests should consider making reservations in advance to secure a table, particularly at popular spots like **Criollo** and **Nois**, known for their exceptional dining experiences.

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-oaxaca/body_6.jpg)
*Photo by [Ali  Alcántara](https://www.pexels.com/@alialcantara) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


When dining in Oaxaca, it’s also a good idea to explore the local specialties. Many restaurants offer unique dishes that highlight the region's culinary heritage, such as mole and tlayudas. Don’t hesitate to ask your server for recommendations or to inquire about the chef's specialties for an authentic taste of Oaxacan cuisine.

 Oaxaca's Michelin-rated restaurants provide a rich mix of flavors and experiences that reflect the city’s culinary heritage. Whether you choose to indulge in a one-star meal or explore the Bib Gourmand offerings, you are sure to find something that resonates with your palate in this culturally rich destination.
