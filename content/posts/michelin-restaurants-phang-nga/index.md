At the pinnacle of Phang-Nga's Michelin offerings is Aulis, which has been awarded one star. Opened in December 2023, Aulis is Simon Rogan's first venture in Thailand, presenting a unique 'chef's table' concept. The focus here is on creative cuisine, where each dish is meticulously crafted to highlight seasonal ingredients and innovative techniques. The ambiance is designed to enhance the dining experience, making it a noteworthy destination for those seeking something extraordinary.

## Bib Gourmand: Best Value Fine Dining

The Bib Gourmand category in Phang-Nga features six establishments that offer excellent food at reasonable prices, making them great choices for those looking to enjoy quality dining without breaking the bank.

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-phang-nga/body_3.jpg)
*Photo by [Siarhei Nester](https://www.pexels.com/@siarhei-nester-318033361) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


Krachang Khao Lak is a seafood and Thai restaurant that boasts a breezy, rustic setting. With a laid-back tropical atmosphere, it combines modern touches with traditional flavors, making it a delightful spot for seafood lovers. 

Tonfon Bistro has been a staple since 1995, where the chef-owner consistently delivers well-presented dishes that showcase the rich flavors of Southern Thai cuisine. The restaurant's longevity speaks to its commitment to quality and authenticity.

Roe Dang provides a more modest dining experience, with basic furnishings that contrast with the skillful execution of its Southern Thai dishes. The restaurant’s focus on precise cooking techniques ensures that each meal is a solid reflection of the region's culinary heritage.

Hok Kee Lao has been serving delicious Thai-Chinese dishes for over 40 years. This heritage restaurant is known for its flavorful offerings that capture the essence of both Thai and Chinese cuisines, making it a cherished spot for locals and visitors alike.

Mon, established for over 35 years, has built strong ties with local fishermen, ensuring that the seafood served is always fresh and of the highest quality. This connection to local sources enhances the authenticity of its Southern Thai dishes.

Krua Luang Ten stands out with its unique setting, featuring tables arranged on sand-covered floors. This casual dining atmosphere complements the delicious Southern Thai dishes it offers, making it a popular choice for both locals and tourists.

## Cuisine Styles You Will Find in Phang-Nga

Phang-Nga's culinary scene is rich and diverse, with a significant emphasis on Southern Thai cuisine. The region's top cuisines include Southern Thai, Thai, Thai-Chinese, and seafood, among others. Each style reflects the local culture and the abundance of fresh ingredients available in the area.

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-phang-nga/body_4.jpg)
*Photo by [Jonny Belvedere](https://www.pexels.com/@jonny-belvedere-1161164) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


Street food is also a prominent aspect of Phang-Nga's food landscape, with several establishments earning recognition for their flavorful and authentic offerings. For instance, Khok Kloi Tom Yam Noodles with Eggs is a humble street stall known for its delicious tom yam noodles, while Bang Dean is popular for its aromatic halal breakfast options.

In addition to traditional fare, Phang-Nga also embraces creative culinary expressions. Aulis exemplifies this with its innovative approach to dining, showcasing how modern techniques can elevate local ingredients.

## How to Book and Tips for Dining

While many of the Michelin-rated restaurants in Phang-Nga welcome walk-in diners, it is advisable to make reservations, especially for popular spots like Aulis. This ensures a smoother dining experience and guarantees a table at busy times.
