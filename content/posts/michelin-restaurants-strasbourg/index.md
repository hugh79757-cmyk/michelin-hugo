Among the Michelin-rated restaurants in Strasbourg, six establishments have earned the prestigious one-star rating, showcasing the city’s commitment to quality and innovation in the culinary arts. 

**Les Funambules** stands out with its modern cuisine, where Chef Guillaume Besson skillfully balances flavors and presentation in a minimalist setting. Diners can expect to pay between €70 and €150 for a meal that reflects both creativity and precision.

In the heart of the picturesque Petite [France](https://visafree.techpawz.com/posts/france-visa-free/) district, **Umami** offers another modern cuisine experience, also priced between €70 and €150. This restaurant is known for its inviting atmosphere, complemented by the charming half-timbered houses that surround it.

**Au Crocodile** is a culinary landmark, recognized for its modern cuisine and long history. The restaurant, with its unique stuffed crocodile display, serves dishes that are both innovative and rooted in tradition, with prices exceeding €150.

For those seeking a more creative approach, **de:ja** presents an extraordinary dining experience. With a price point over €150, this restaurant features a Scandi-inspired decor that reflects its innovative culinary techniques, including extractions and fermentations.

**1741**, located opposite Palais Rohan, is another modern cuisine restaurant that offers a very expensive dining experience, also over €150. Its name pays homage to the year the architectural masterpiece was completed, making it a fitting location for a memorable meal.

Lastly, **Guillaume Scheer - Les Plaisirs Gourmands** combines modern cuisine with a serene riverside setting. This very expensive option, priced above €150, highlights Chef Scheer’s dedication to quality and his unique culinary vision.

## Bib Gourmand: Best Value Fine Dining

Strasbourg is home to two Bib Gourmand establishments, which signify exceptional quality at more affordable prices. 

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-strasbourg/body_3.jpg)
*Photo by [Ramon Karolan](https://www.pexels.com/@ramonkaphotography) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


**Au Pont Corbeau** provides a delightful introduction to Alsatian cuisine, where guests can enjoy local food specialties in a setting adorned with Renaissance features. With a moderate price range of €30 to €70, this restaurant is perfect for those looking to experience authentic flavors without breaking the bank.

Similarly, **Chez Yvonne - S'Burjerstuewel** captures the essence of Alsatian country cooking. With a lively atmosphere and closely packed tables, this winstub offers a menu that embodies traditional Alsatian dishes, also falling within the moderate price range of €30 to €70.

## Cuisine Styles You Will Find in Strasbourg

Strasbourg’s Michelin dining scene is characterized by a rich variety of cuisine styles. The most prominent is modern cuisine, which is represented by 14 of the Michelin-rated restaurants. This style often emphasizes creativity and innovation, with chefs experimenting with textures, flavors, and presentation.

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-strasbourg/body_4.jpg)
*Photo by [Connor Kelley](https://www.pexels.com/@connorkelley) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


Alsatian cuisine is another key element of Strasbourg’s culinary identity, with two Michelin-rated restaurants dedicated to this traditional fare. These establishments offer a taste of the region's rich culinary heritage, showcasing local ingredients and classic recipes.

In addition to modern and Alsatian styles, the city features a selection of creative dining options, classic cuisine, and even Italian influences, ensuring that there is something to suit every palate.

## Price Ranges and What to Expect

The Michelin-rated restaurants in Strasbourg span a range of price points, catering to different budgets and dining preferences. 

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-strasbourg/body_5.jpg)
*Photo by [Cem Kurtuldu](https://www.pexels.com/@cem-kurtuldu-2038531239) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


At the high end, establishments like **1741**, **Au Crocodile**, and **de:ja** feature very expensive menus, typically exceeding €150. Guests can expect a refined dining experience with meticulously crafted dishes that highlight the chefs' expertise and creativity.

Mid-range options, such as **Les Funambules** and **Umami**, offer meals priced between €70 and €150. These restaurants provide a balance of quality and affordability, making them suitable for those looking to indulge without venturing into the highest price brackets.

For those on a tighter budget, the Bib Gourmand selections, **Au Pont Corbeau** and **Chez Yvonne - S'Burjerstuewel**, offer excellent value with prices ranging from €30 to €70. These restaurants focus on delivering satisfying meals that reflect the local culinary culture.

## How to Book and Tips for Dining

When planning to dine at one of Strasbourg's Michelin-rated restaurants, it is advisable to make reservations in advance, especially for the more popular establishments. Many restaurants offer online booking options, while others may require a phone call for reservations.

![Photo](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/michelin-restaurants-strasbourg/body_6.jpg)
*Photo by [Florian Süß](https://www.pexels.com/@florian-suss-269098632) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*


Dress codes can vary, so it is wise to check in advance. Generally, smart casual attire is appropriate for most Michelin-rated venues. Arriving on time is also important, as these restaurants often have tight seating schedules.

Finally, take the time to explore the menu options and consider trying a tasting menu if available. This allows diners to experience a range of dishes and flavors, showcasing the chef's talents and the restaurant's culinary philosophy.

 Strasbourg's Michelin dining scene offers a diverse and exciting array of culinary experiences. From the innovative modern cuisine to the comforting traditional Alsatian dishes, the city presents a rich mix of flavors and styles that are sure to satisfy any food lover's cravings.
